## Overview
Bikash Kushwaha's working pattern reads as a **builder-investigator**: self-directed, evidence-driven and careful about honesty in results. He picks real problems, digs into why data misleads, and writes up what broke as carefully as what worked.

## Core Behavioral Drives

| Drive | Level | Meaning |
|-------|-------|---------|
| Autonomy / self-direction | High (inferred) | Starts and finishes large projects without external structure |
| Rigor / attention to detail | High (inferred) | Validates data flags, lineage and test failures before trusting results |
| Social impact | High (inferred) | Chooses problems that matter for people in Nepal (disasters, air, payments, farmers) |
| Pace | Steady, sustained (inferred) | Ships a series of complete, documented projects |

## Strongest Behaviors
- **Honest reporting:** states what is simulated, not deployed or noisy before being asked; reports held-out results separately.
- **Root-cause thinking:** traces surprising numbers to their cause (zeros that mean "not reported", re-keyed IDs, cancelling errors).
- **Cost and failure awareness:** designs each system with a monthly cost, failure modes and alarms for silence.

## How You Work Best
- Ownership of a problem end to end, from data to deployed service
- Teams that value measurement, written design decisions and post-mortems
- Remote or hybrid work with clear goals and room to investigate

## Growth Areas (frame positively in applications)
- **Production tenure:** short formal work history (3-month cloud role, short data analyst stint). Frame: a portfolio of eleven end-to-end systems, each with costs, ADRs and bug write-ups, shows production thinking; eager to run them at real scale.
- **Large-team experience:** most work is solo. Frame: every project is documented for a reader, ready for review and handover.

## Mapping to Job Posting Language

When a job posting mentions these keywords, it's a **strong behavioral fit**:
- "ownership", "end-to-end", "self-starter", "data quality", "reliability", "monitoring"
- "impact", "public sector", "climate", "fintech", "build from scratch"

When a job posting mentions these, flag as **potential friction** (not deal-breaker):
- "mostly maintenance", "ticket queue", "support rotation only"
- "10+ years required", "people management"

## Management Style Preferences
- Works well with: clear outcomes, trust to investigate, technical feedback on design docs
- Less suited to: micromanagement, output measured by hours instead of results

## Using This in Applications
- **Cover letters:** lead with one concrete project story and a number, then the honest caveat; show care for users.
- **CV:** projects with measured results and costs; AWS certifications; TekBay infrastructure work.
- **Interviews:** bug stories from CHALLENGES_AND_BUGS.md (see 07-interview-prep.md).
- **Don't overstate:** never say a stack "runs in production" unless deployed; don't claim PyTorch or Kubernetes depth; say simulated data is simulated.
